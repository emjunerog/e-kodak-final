## 🔄 Flowchart Steps & Data

* **Start**
* **Client Acquisition**
  * Visit School/Online or Walk Inquiry
* **Offer Services and Packages**
  * Pictorial packages, ID slings, Lanyards, etc.
* **Distribute Order Form/ Recieve Order Request**
* **Client Selects Package / Services**
* **Record Order Details & Take Down Payment**
* **Issue Reciept and Verify Order**
* **Types of Services?**
  * **Pictorial Sessions** → Capture Photos
  * **Printing Production** → Print, Heat press, etc.
  * **Editing and Processing**
* **Printing Framing and Quality Check**
* **Notify Client for Completion**
* **Full payment received?**
  * **NO:**
    * Hold Release
    * Send Payment Reminder
  * **YES:**
    * Release Products and Photos
    * Delivery or Client Pickup
* **End**
